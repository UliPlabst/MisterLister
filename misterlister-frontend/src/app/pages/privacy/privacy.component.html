2. What Data We Collect

We collect minimal data to provide the Service. The types of data processed are:

2.1 Data You Provide

When using the Service, you may create TODO lists and items. This may include:

  • Task titles
  • Task descriptions
  • User chosen pseudonym
  • Any text you voluntarily enter into a list

Important: Do not store sensitive personal data (e.g., health, financial, political, or identity information) in your TODO lists.

2.2 System Data

The system stores only the following technical information:

  • List identifier (GUID)
  • Creation and modified timestamps

We do not collect or store:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or user accounts
  • IP addresses or device identifiers
  • Location data
  • Cookies or tracking identifiers
  • Analytics or profiling data

2.3 Pseudonymization

Users are not identified with user accounts. Any identifier used by the system is a pseudonymous string that cannot directly identify a person without additional information.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only for the following purposes:

  • To store and display your TODO lists and items
  • To allow access via a GUID-based link
  • To allow users to edit or delete their own lists
  • To ensure technical operation of the Service

We do not:

  • Sell data
  • Share data with advertisers
  • Perform tracking or profiling
  • Use data for marketing
  • Use data for automated decision-making

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under GDPR Article 6, we process your data based on:

a) Performance of a Contract (Art. 6(1)(b))

Processing is necessary to provide the TODO service you requested.

b) Legitimate Interest (Art. 6(1)(f))

We have a legitimate interest in operating a minimal and privacy-friendly TODO service.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not share your data with third parties except:

  • Hosting provider (for server storage only, under a data-processing agreement)
  • Authorities, if legally required

We do not transfer your data outside the EU unless adequate protection mechanisms are in place.

6. Data Retention

  • Data is kept until you delete your list.
  • Deleted lists are permanently removed.
  • No backups are kept beyond technical necessity.
  • Orphaned lists may be automatically removed after a reasonable time.

7. Your Rights Under GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Rectify incorrect data
  • Erase your data (“right to be forgotten”)
  • Restrict processing
  • Object to processing
  • Data portability (where technically feasible)

Since we do not identify users directly, access or erasure is performed via possession of the list GUID.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority (DPA).

8. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS)
  • Restricted database access
  • Limited data storage by design (privacy by design)

However, no system is 100% secure.
